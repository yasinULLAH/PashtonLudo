📖 Bannu & Pashtun Cultural Ludo Rules (بنوں کے روایتی لڈو قوانین)

Every regional rule from your voice notes is built into the game engine and can be toggled individually or tested live via the Cultural Rule Drills.

1. Two-Dice Tactical Play (دو گوٹیوں کا کھیل)

Roll 1 or 2 dice. With 2 dice (e.g. 5 & 3), you can combine both values (8 steps) on a single bead or split them (5 on one bead, 3 on another). Rolling Double Six (6+6) grants a bonus turn!

2. Mandatory Hand Spawn on 6 (پہلے ہاتھ والی بیڈ لگانا)

If you still have beads waiting in your hand/yard and roll a 6 (e.g. 6 and 1), you cannot move your existing ground bead with that 6—you must first spawn a bead from your hand onto the board, then play the remaining 1.

3. Kill Required for Home + Full Board Loop (بغیر شکار اندر جانا منع)

You cannot enter your colored home stretch until you capture at least 1 opponent bead. Even if you only have 1 bead on the board and reach your home gate without a kill, you must turn past it and complete another full 52-square lap!

4. 2v2 Teams & Single-Capture Choice (ٹیم میچ اور ایک شکار کا انتخاب)

Opposite corners are teammates (Red+Yellow vs Green+Blue). Teammates never kill each other and can stand together on any square. If an enemy lands on two stacked teammates on an unprotected square, they can only eat one bead of their choice!

5. Close Game vs Open Game (کلوز گیم بمقابلہ اوپن گیم)

In Close Game, when your bead captures an enemy, that capturing bead freezes on that spot for the rest of your turn if another bead can legally move or spawn. In Open Game, you may continue moving the capturing bead immediately.

6. Strict Near-Home Remainder & Partner Assist (بقایا نمبر اور ٹیم ہیلپ)

If you have only 1 bead on the ground needing 6 for Home and roll 5+4 (9), you cannot play because the remaining 4 has nowhere to go. In 2v2, once you finish all your beads, you roll 1 Die until you get a 6—unlocking 2-Dice control of your partner's beads!

⚔️ Choose Which Enemy Bead to Capture!

Multiple enemy team beads are stacked on this unprotected square. Under Bannu Team Rules, you may only eat ONE bead of your choice and leave the other standing!
