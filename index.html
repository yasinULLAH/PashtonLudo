📖 Bannu & Pashtun Cultural Rules

1. Two-Dice Tactical Play & Kill Re-Roll

With 2 dice (e.g. 5 & 3), drag or tap a bead to move by one die or combine both dice on one bead. Rolling Double Six (6+6) OR eating/killing an enemy bead grants a bonus re-roll!

2. Mandatory Hand Spawn on 6 (پہلے ہاتھ والی بیڈ لگانا)

If you have beads in your hand/yard and roll a 6 (e.g. 6+1), you must spawn a bead from your hand first before moving existing board beads.

3. Kill Required for Home + Loop (بغیر شکار اندر جانا منع)

Cannot enter the home column without capturing at least 1 enemy bead. Reaching home entry with 0 kills forces another full 52-square lap!

4. 2v2 Teams & Single-Capture Choice (ٹیم میچ)

Opposite corners are teammates (Red+Yellow ♦T1 vs Green+Blue ★T2). Teammates stack safely; an enemy landing on stacked teammates can only eat 1 chosen bead.

5. Close Game vs Open Game (کلوز گیم بمقابلہ اوپن گیم)

In Close Game, a bead that kills an enemy locks on that square for the rest of your turn if another bead can move or spawn.

6. Disconnect / Close Protection & AI Takeover

Every turn is auto-saved so closing or refreshing the app lets you resume immediately. If a player loses connection or leaves mid-match, an intelligent AI Bot automatically takes over their seat!
