Pashtun Pakistani KPK Area Rules & Custom Board Builder

Authentic Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Hujra regulations with a full interactive Snake & Ladder coordinate designer.

1. Dakhila & 3x6 Barsi Rule

Tokens wait in Hujra Camp (0) until a 6 (Chakka) is rolled. Each 6 grants a bonus roll, but rolling three consecutive 6s (6-6-6) burns the turn ("Teen Chakkay Barsi") and reverts the token to its start-of-turn state.

2. KPK Takkar (Knockback Cut)

Landing on an opponent's square triggers a Takkar (Cut), sending them back to Square 1! Designated Hujra Safe Zones (1, 14, 27, 50, 75, 92) grant full immunity from knockbacks.

3. Square 99 Kalamar & Pura 100

Square 99 hosts the dreaded Kalamar (Black Cobra) dropping players down to Square 4. Reaching Square 100 requires an Exact Roll ("Pura 100").
