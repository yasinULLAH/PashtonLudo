Pashtun Pakistani KPK Area Rules & Custom Presets

Authentic Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Hujra regulations alongside custom board snake/ladder presets.

1. Dakhila & 3x6 Barsi Rule

In KPK Hujra rules, a player's token stays off-board in Camp (0) until a 6 (Chakka) is rolled. Each 6 grants a bonus roll, but rolling three consecutive 6s (6-6-6) burns the entire turn ("Teen Chakkay Barsi") and reverts the token to its start-of-turn square.

2. KPK Takkar (Knockback Cut)

Landing on a square occupied by an opponent triggers a Takkar (Cut), sending the opponent's token back to Square 1! However, designated Hujra Safe Zones (1, 14, 27, 50, 75, 92) protect all tokens resting on them from being knocked back.

3. Square 99 Kalamar & Pura 100

Square 99 hosts the dreaded Kalamar (Black Cobra) dropping players down to Square 4. Reaching Square 100 requires an Exact Roll ("Pura 100"); overshooting keeps the token in place for the next turn.
